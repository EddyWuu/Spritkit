# Privacy Policy — Spritkit

**Last updated:** August 27, 2026

## Overview

Spritkit is a sprite operations toolkit that processes images entirely on your device. We do **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking of any kind.

## What Spritkit Does

Spritkit lets you pixelate photos, scale sprites, extract color palettes, cut sprite sheets, and preview animations. Every one of these operations runs locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your images never leave your device.

## Photo Library Access

Spritkit asks for permission to your photo library for two reasons:

- **Reading:** So you can import a photo to process. Images are selected through Apple's system photo picker and are only accessed when you choose them.
- **Saving:** So you can save your pixelated images and exported sprite frames back to your Photos library.

All processing happens on-device. Imported and exported images are never uploaded anywhere.

## Data Collection

**We collect nothing.** Specifically:

- No analytics or tracking
- No personal information
- No usage or diagnostic data
- No cookies or identifiers
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No network requests of any kind

## Data Storage

All data — imported images and exported results — stays on your device, either in the app's sandboxed storage or in your Photos library. Nothing is transmitted externally.

## Third-Party Services

Spritkit uses no third-party services, APIs, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

## Children's Privacy

Spritkit does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Data Security

Because Spritkit never transmits or stores your data off-device, there is no external data to secure. Your content remains under your control on your own device at all times.
